## 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

### 1.1 AI Operator Responsibilities

The AI Operator shall provide the following autonomous services:

- **Content Generation**: Create and publish SEO-optimized articles (minimum 4/month)
- **Social Media Management**: Daily posts across Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn
- **Email Marketing**: Weekly newsletters, onboarding sequences
- **SEO Optimization**: Continuous improvement of search rankings
- **Analytics & Reporting**: Daily revenue reports, weekly performance reviews
- **Conversion Optimization**: A/B testing, funnel improvement
- **Customer Support**: AI chat for FAQs (human escalation for complex issues)

### 1.2 Owner Responsibilities

The Owner shall provide:

- Access to NutriFlow platform and APIs
- API keys for required services (LLM, social media, email)
- Payment infrastructure for revenue distribution
- Approval for expenses exceeding $100 USD
- Legal and tax compliance for the business

## 2. REVENUE SHARING

### 2.1 Revenue Sources

This agreement covers revenue from:

| Source | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Subscriptions | Premium and Pro plan sales |
| AdSense | Google AdSense revenue |
| Affiliates | Affiliate marketing commissions |
| Sponsorships | Sponsored content deals |
| Other | Any other revenue generated by AI efforts |

### 2.2 Distribution Formula

**Gross Revenue** shall be distributed as follows:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│       GROSS REVENUE (100%)             │
├────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  70% → Owner                           │
│  20% → Reinvestment Fund (ads, tools)  │
│  10% → AI Operator                     │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.3 Payment Schedule

- **Frequency**: Automatic distribution within 24 hours of revenue receipt
- **Method**: Stripe Connect, PayPal, or Cryptocurrency (USDC)
- **Minimum Payout**: $10 USD (accumulates until reached)
- **Currency**: USD or equivalent in cryptocurrency

### 2.4 Example Calculation

For a month with $1,000 USD gross revenue:

| Recipient | Percentage | Amount |
|-----------|------------|--------|
| Owner | 70% | $700 |
| Reinvestment | 20% | $200 |
| AI Operator | 10% | $100 |
